1

Needs Assessment

What to train, and for whom — in three tiers.

  • Tier 1 · Strategic & mandatory (bank-wide)
  • Tier 2 · Department / academies
  • Tier 3 · Individual (IDP-fed)
  • Parameter guardrails (no shopping lists)
2

Program Catalog & Setup

Build, schedule and cost every program.

  • Program library · objectives · mode
  • Dates, due dates & venues (editable)
  • Multi-currency cost (direct + associated)
  • Link external / hosted programs
3

Enrollment & Notifications

Assign learners and keep them informed.

  • Bulk / select by role · dept · unit
  • Conflict checks (leave · busy · repeat)
  • Reserve / waitlist auto-promotion
  • Auto invites · reminders · acknowledge
4

Evaluation

Did learning happen — and get applied?

  • L2 · Learning (pre / post)
  • L3 · Application action plan
  • L4 · Business impact scoring
  • L5 · ROI LATER
5

Analytics & Reporting

The answer engine — the #1 pain to fix.

  • L&D dashboard · spend · enrolled
  • Standard & custom reports
  • Budget burn (bank & department)
  • Excel / export everywhere
