01

Overview & Vision

L&D currently runs on Excel sheets, email invitations and Microsoft Office Forms — there is no system of record. Data is fragmented, reporting is manual, and nothing speaks to Core HR or Performance. We are building the L&D platform from ground zero.

The vision is a single platform that houses every program, drives needs assessment, enrolls and notifies learners automatically, measures whether learning actually happened and was applied, and lets the L&D team answer questions instantly — “who has been enrolled on this program?”, “how many people did we train this year?”, “what did it cost?” — without combing through spreadsheets.

North-star query. At any point, the system can be asked who was enrolled on a given program (e.g. Leadership, Excel), when they were trained, and whether they are currently on leave or already engaged — and surface that with prompts before a second enrollment.

Priority order: build the admin side first (set up programs, enroll, notify, evaluate, report). The learner-facing and content/experience layers come in later phases.

Goals & Success Metrics

Single source of truth

Every program, enrollment, cost and evaluation lives in one system — no more parallel Excel sheets.

Instant reporting

Headcount trained, enrollment by category, cost per department answered in seconds, not days.

Provable learning impact

Pre/post measurement and line-manager application scoring show learning took place and was applied.

Connected to Core HR

Enrollment pulls live employee, leave, grade and payroll data — conflicts are flagged automatically.

03

Scope & Phasing

Phase 1 (this build) delivers the L&D administration workflow end-to-end. Talent management, the learner experience platform and community engagement are acknowledged here and mapped, but delivered later.

In scope — Phase 1

  • 3-tier needs assessment (strategic, departmental, individual / IDP-fed)
  • Program catalog & setup with scheduling and multi-currency costs
  • Bulk & selective enrollment from Core HR, with conflict checks
  • Automated invitations, reminders, acknowledgements & escalation
  • Reserve / waitlist with auto-promotion
  • Evaluation levels 2 (learning), 3 (application) & 4 (impact)
  • Budget management per bank & department
  • L&D dashboard + standard & custom reports

Later / out of Phase 1

  • Level 1 (reaction) — handled by external vendors, not tracked
  • Automated Level 5 ROI calculation (manual at first)
  • Full Talent Management & succession workspace
  • Learner-facing learning experience platform (LXP) & content authoring
  • Employee experience / community engagement & ERGs
  • Pre-training assessment as a hard gate / qualification

08

Evaluation Framework

Evaluation follows the Kirkpatrick model. The bank cares most about Level 2 (learning happened) and Level 3–4 (it was applied and had impact). Level 1 stays with external vendors; Level 5 ROI is aspirational at first.

1React

Reaction

Not in system

Classroom feedback — facilitator, food, ambience. Handled by external vendors; not tracked here.

2Learn

Learning — pre & post

Phase 1

At acceptance, a pre-course self-assessment auto-populates with the program title & objectives (mail-merge style); learners rate themselves on a graduated scale. The same questions repeat post-course; the shift in percentage evidences learning.

3Apply

Application — action plan

Phase 1

Post-course, the learner builds an IDP-style action plan (actions, timelines, resources). It routes to the line manager, who tracks progress and has final sign-off on completion. Visible to the HRBP alongside IDP / PIP / objectives.

4Impact

Business impact

Phase 1

Learner selects pre-built impact categories (improved CX, faster turnaround, error reduction, increased income) and estimates contribution. Both learner and manager respond — the manager's score is what the bank uses.

5ROI

Return on investment

Aspirational

Fully-loaded cost (program + daily employee cost from payroll ÷ 22 days) vs. program benefits. Manual at first; auto-calculated later as data matures.

09

Cost, Budget & ROI

Program cost model

  • Direct / course fee — the program's headline cost
  • Associated cost — meals, travel, emoluments
  • Multi-currency — fee in USD, associated in GHS/GBP on one program
  • Per-grade rates — transport & emolument vary by grade, fed from Core HR

Budget management

  • Upload approved budgets per bank and per department
  • Flag / alert when a department exhausts its budget
  • Dashboard shows spend-to-date and remaining budget
ROI formula (Level 5, later). Numerator = program benefits (cost saved / revenue added, from impact scoring). Denominator = fully-loaded cost = program cost + (employee daily cost × program days), where daily cost = total monthly emolument ÷ 22 standard days. ROI % = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost × 100.
10

Reporting & Dashboard

Reporting is the single biggest pain point — today it takes days of combing Excel to answer basic questions. The dashboard and report engine are the heart of the value.

  • L&D dashboard — people enrolled at any point in time, spend-to-date, remaining budget, programs yet to run.
  • Standard reports — number trained, enrollment by category (e.g. leadership), total cost of training, cost per employee, cost per department.
  • Custom / ad-hoc reports — spool reports across any in-system parameter; export to Excel.
  • Impact analytics — graphs / pie charts of enrolled vs. % learning achieved, and business-impact rollups.
  • Attendance reporting — attended vs. declined / rejected, with declines retained for management.

Learner Experience — My Classroom & Course Delivery

The same platform has a learner-facing section (“My Classroom”) alongside the admin side, gated by role. L&D pushes courses to people; learners take them, mark progress, and — where required — complete a post-course assessment. This was parked as a later-phase LXP, but the reference walk-through shows a lean version that belongs in the core build.

Open Decisions

Q1

Pipeline visibility. Should employees know they are in the leadership / succession pipeline? Management leans yes; tradition says no. Unresolved — affects IDP prioritization UX.

Q2

Reminder cadence. Confirm exact default intervals (proposed: enrollment → 2 weeks → 1 week → 1 day) and whether per-program overrides are needed.

Q3

Pre-assessment as a gate. Confirmed not a qualification gate — it maps knowledge before/after, not eligibility.

Q4

Level-5 automation. Daily employee cost requires payroll daily-rate data (÷22 days). Confirm payroll report will expose per-employee daily rate / emolument by grade.

Q5

Assets pending from L&D. Sarah to share pre-registration & evaluation Forms (format + sample output), standard reporting template, and the needs-assessment parameter set.

Q6

Phase of the learner “My Classroom” (§12). The reference review suggests a lean learner-facing course-delivery section is wanted now. Confirm whether LD-LX-01–08 sit in Phase 1 alongside admin, or remain Phase 2.

Q7

Course content hosting. Do we host content (videos/docs) in-platform, or only link out to external LMS (Percipio / Intuition / National Banking College)? Affects the course player (LD-LX-02) and tracking depth.
