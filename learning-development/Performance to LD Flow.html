Individual Development Plan (IDP)

Built /appraisals/development-plan

Set before the year begins · reviewed through it

— the bridge from performance into learning

Each development goal

Every employee sets 3 to 5 goals. Each carries a type and priority, then breaks down into activities, and each activity names a learning method.

Type · Technical Type · Behavioral Type · Leadership Priority · High / Med / Low

Manager accepts the plan, then reviews progress at two checkpoints:

Accept→ Mid-year review→ End-year review

Learning methods · the 70-20-10 model

70%
On-the-job experience

Stretch assignments, job rotation, acting roles, leading projects.

20%
Social learning

Mentoring, coaching, peer groups, 360° feedback, communities of practice.

10%
Formal training

Courses, certifications, workshops, e-learning, leadership programmes.

The Handoff into Learning & Development

Built → In design /appraisals/idp-management → L&D

IDP demand becomes the L&D demand signal

HRBPs see every development plan in one overview — who's done, who hasn't, and what each person chose. Aggregated across the bank, those individual choices become L&D's procurement and planning input. “500 people want formal training in X; 200 want to understudy…”

10%

Formal training demand

Courses & certifications to source, schedule and cost → L&D Program Catalog.

20%

Coaching & mentoring

Pairings to set up and oversee → L&D coaching workflow.

70%

On-the-job experiences

Rotations & stretch assignments tracked against impact.
