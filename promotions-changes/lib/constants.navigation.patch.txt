WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Groups the existing "Promotions" and "Transfers" links under a single
"People & Culture" section, exactly the way "Core HR", "Leave Management"
and "Employee Engagement" are structured (showSubItemsAsTabs: true).

• Sidebar: one "People & Culture" entry under HR Management.
• In-page: a tab bar [Promotions | Transfers] is rendered automatically by
  components/layout/Header.tsx (findItemWithTabs) — NO per-page changes needed.
• Icons used (Users, BriefcaseBusinessIcon, ArrowRightLeft) are ALREADY
  imported in this file. No new imports required.

NOTES
-----
• `href: ROUTES.PROMOTIONS.MAIN` on the parent is intentional — Header.tsx /
  SidebarMenuItem.tsx navigate a tabbed parent to its first sub-item.
• The tab bar matches the active route by EXACT path (subItem.href === pathname),
  so the tabs highlight on /promotions and /transfers (consistent with the
  other sections; deep pages like /promotions/create don't show the bar).
• Permissions: if your sidebar filters by permission (useAccessibleNavigation),
  the "People & Culture" group inherits visibility from its sub-items, so it
  appears whenever Promotions or Transfers is permitted — no extra config.
